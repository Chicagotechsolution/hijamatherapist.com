Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

This is a pre-opening website. It collects no personal information, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. We take the privacy of anything health-adjacent seriously — which is why, for now, we simply collect nothing.

The form on this site

Our interest form does not transmit anything. What you type — including any question about your health — stays in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page. Before any form here begins sending data, this policy will be rewritten first to explain exactly what is collected, how it is protected, and how long it is kept.

Fonts

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google's servers. Nothing else is loaded from third parties.

Changes

Any change to these practices will be posted here with a new date before it takes effect.
